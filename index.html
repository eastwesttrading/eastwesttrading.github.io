Who We Are

East West Grinding or Manufacturing of Grains PLC is an Ethiopian company specializing in agro-processing, export, and import. We source high-quality agricultural products directly from Ethiopian farmers, process them using modern cleaning and grading technologies, and export them to international markets.

Our commitment to quality, integrity, customer satisfaction, and timely delivery has made us a reliable partner for buyers worldwide.
